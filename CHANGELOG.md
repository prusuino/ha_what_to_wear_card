# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-27

- **New style: emoji.** A big round emoji head on the friendly body: it grins in the sun, frowns in the rain and shivers in the frost. Classic emoji yellow unless you choose a skin; hair, beards, glasses and headwear work as in the other styles.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-25

First release.

- One person per card, dressed for the weather of the coming hours, from any weather entity.
- Five styles: friendly, street comic, pictogram, black and white, clothes as symbols.
- The person is yours to choose: age, gender, skin, hair, beard, headwear, glasses and the colours of the clothes.
- Advice in words, in English, German, French and Italian, in °C and °F.
- Set up in the editor of the card; `demo` shows it with invented weather.
